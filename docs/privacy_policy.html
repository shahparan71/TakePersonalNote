This policy explains how the App stores, accesses, uses, protects, and handles information when you use its features.

2. Information Collection and Local Storage

The App allows you to create and manage personal notes, calendar entries, tasks, reminders, and application settings. By default, this information is stored locally on your device using the App's local database.

The Developer does not receive your locally stored notes or tasks on a server operated by the Developer, unless a feature explicitly transfers information as described in this policy.

3. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to use the optional Google Drive backup and restore feature, the App may access your Google account and transfer backup data to Google Drive.

  • Authentication: Google Sign-In may be used to authenticate your Google account.
  • Access: The App requests only the Google Drive access scope configured for its backup feature. If configured to use app-specific storage, backup files are stored in the App's dedicated Google Drive area.
  • Purpose: Backup data is used to restore your information when you choose to do so.
  • Third parties: Google processes account information and data as necessary to provide its services under Google's applicable privacy policies.

Google Drive backup is optional. Its availability and the information transferred depend on your use of this feature.

4. Biometric Authentication and Security

The App may offer fingerprint or face authentication to help protect access to your notes. Biometric verification is handled by your device's operating system. The App does not directly collect or store your raw fingerprint or facial data.

Any password protection or encryption described by the App applies only to the extent implemented by the installed version. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

5. Permissions

  • Photos, media, or file access: May be required to select attachments, export files, or perform supported local backup operations.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver reminders that you configure, subject to your device's settings and permission requirements.
  • Network access: Used when online functionality, including Google Drive backup, requires it.

Actual permissions depend on your device, Android version, and the features enabled in the App.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Data Sharing

The App does not include third-party advertising frameworks or sell your personal information, as long as these statements accurately describe the released version.

When you use Google Drive integration, information is processed by Google as needed to provide that functionality. The App's actual third-party SDKs and services, if any, must also be reflected in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored notes, tasks, and related information remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to your device's storage behavior and any copies you have exported.

If you use Google Drive backup, backed-up information may remain in Google Drive until you delete the relevant backup files. Deleting local information does not necessarily delete an existing cloud backup, and deleting a cloud backup does not necessarily delete local information.

You can contact the Developer using the email below for privacy-related questions or requests concerning information handled by the Developer.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through a Developer-operated server. If you believe information has been provided to the Developer inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
